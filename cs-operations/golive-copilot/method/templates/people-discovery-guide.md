# People discovery guide

Short, one-to-one voice interviews with individual staff, done before configuration starts. The workflow call tells me how work is meant to happen. These interviews tell me how it really happens, and how people feel about the AI agent.

## Why voice

People speak more freely than they type. A five-minute conversation gets the hesitations, the workarounds and the "to be honest" moments that a survey never does. It's also quicker for staff who spend their day on the phone or with customers.

## Set-up

- **Who:** 5 to 10 people across every site and role: reception or front desk, managers, the people who handle urgent requests, and at least one person who isn't keen. Ask the champion who people go to for answers, and include them.
- **How long:** 5 to 10 minutes each.
- **Recording:** only with the person's consent, asked at the start of the recording. If they say no, take written notes instead.
- **Notes:** made with an AI note-taker the business has approved. If it hasn't approved one, take notes by hand.
- **Privacy:** tell each person that their words won't be quoted to managers by name without their agreement. Themes are shared; names are not.
- **Storage:** keep recordings only as long as the business's retention policy allows, then delete them.

## Opening script

"Thanks for making time. I'm helping set up the AI agent that will answer some of your calls and messages. I'd like to hear how your work goes today, so we set it up around how you actually work. It's about ten minutes. Is it OK if I record this so I don't miss anything? It's only used for my notes, and I won't quote you by name to anyone without asking."

## Questions

1. **What you do today.** Walk me through a normal day. Where do calls and messages come from, and what do you do with them?
2. **Where requests fall through.** When does something get missed, or answered late? What happens then?
3. **What you'd hand over gladly.** Which parts of the job would you be happy never to do again?
4. **What worries you about the AI agent.** What's the worst thing that could happen? Is anything about it worrying you personally?
5. **Who people go to for answers.** When you don't know something, who do you ask? Is there anything only one person knows?

Follow-ups: "Can you give me an example?" and "What happened next?"

## After each interview

Write up within the day, under these headings:

| Heading | What to note |
|---|---|
| Role and site | |
| How work flows today | Channels, volumes, workarounds |
| Leak points | Where requests are missed or late |
| Happy to hand over | Tasks |
| Worries | In their words, paraphrased |
| Go-to people | Who they ask, and what only one person knows |
| Signals | Enthusiastic, influential, sceptical, worried about their job |

Then update `people-risk-register.md` and `champion-map.md`.
