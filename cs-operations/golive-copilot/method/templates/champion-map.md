# Champion map

The org chart says who decides. The champion map shows who people actually follow. I build it from the discovery interviews, especially the question "who do you go to for answers?".

## The three things I look for

- **Enthusiastic:** wants it to work and will try it first.
- **Influential:** others copy what they do or listen to what they say.
- **Go-to person:** the one people already ask when they don't know something.

The best champion is all three. The riskiest person is influential and sceptical.

## Map

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | Yes / Mixed / No | Yes / No | Yes / No | Keen / Neutral / Worried / Sceptical | Champion, reviewer, tester, site lead, keep informed |

## How to use it

- **Champion:** enthusiastic, influential and ideally the go-to person. Signs off the overflow stage. Gets a direct line to the go-live lead.
- **Reviewer:** the go-to person or single point of knowledge. Checks the knowledge base and the shadow-stage drafts.
- **Tester:** sceptics, especially influential ones. Ask them to try to break it before go-live.
- **Site lead:** one per site, so no site depends on a champion somewhere else.

## Coverage check

- Every site has someone in the "enthusiastic" column.
- Every influential sceptic has a named action in `people-risk-register.md`.
- The champion isn't also the only escalation contact.
