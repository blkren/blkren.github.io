# Role-change message for staff

A short, honest message from the business's decision maker to staff, sent before the shadow stage. I draft it; the decision maker edits it and sends it in their own name.

## Rules for the message

- **Acknowledge the worry about jobs directly.** Don't wait to be asked.
- **Be honest that roles change.** Don't say "nothing will change".
- **Say what stays human.**
- **Reframe the value:** more time for the work only people can do (relationships, visits, complex cases) and less admin.
- **Never promise outcomes the business hasn't committed to.** If the business hasn't decided something, such as hours or headcount, don't say anything about it. Mark it `[MISSING: decision needed]` in the draft and ask the decision maker.
- Plain words. Under 250 words. Follow up in person.

## Template

> **Subject: The AI agent, and what it means for your role**
>
> From [date], we're starting to use an AI agent to answer some of our calls and messages, starting with [first stage, for example evenings and weekends at one site].
>
> I know some of you are wondering whether this is about jobs. I'd rather say it plainly than leave you guessing. [Business's actual commitment, for example "We are not reducing the team because of this." Only include if the business has committed to it.]
>
> Your role will change. The AI agent will take on [the repeat work, for example answering routine questions and booking straightforward appointments]. That means less time on the phone chasing [admin], and more time for the work only people can do: [for this business, for example looking after patients in clinic, showing people round properties, dealing with complex cases].
>
> Some things will always come to a person: [always-human list, for example complaints, payments, anything urgent or sensitive].
>
> We're rolling it out in stages, and it only moves on when it's proved itself. You'll see its answers before customers do, and your feedback decides when it's ready. [Champion name] is our lead on this, and you can talk to them or to me at any time.
>
> [Decision maker name]

## Follow-up

- A 15-minute team huddle at each site within a week of the message.
- Repeat the "what stays human" list at each stage gate.
- Track the fear of replacement risk (14) in the people risk register, and re-rate it after the huddles.
